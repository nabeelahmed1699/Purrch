# Purrch

macOS notch pet app. Pixel-art cat that lives in the MacBook notch.

## Rules

- **Never exceed the notch height unless the mouse is hovering over the notch.** Without hover, the black shape (and everything drawn in it) stays exactly the notch's height; it may only grow sideways into the menu bar. Taller layouts (e.g. the big chair cat) are allowed only while hovered.
- The notch area itself has no display pixels: anything drawn inside the notch rectangle is invisible on the real screen. Treat it as a tunnel/hiding place, never as a place to show content.
